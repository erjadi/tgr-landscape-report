Landscape reconstruction / four rally stages / 27 October 2026 Verified on Spain and deployed

From a repeated corridor
to a surveyed landscape.

Coherent woodland, real openings, source-located landmarks and a camera that anticipates the canopy. Evidence first: fixed-view renders, correctly sampled motion and an explicit account of what remains invented.

01 / What changed

Geographic variety, not randomness.

The original stage assets contained useful terrain, aerial imagery and classified returns, but the roadside world was largely organized by the same synthetic openness field, road-index scenery loops and a shared green ground treatment. Increasing density everywhere would have made that repetition worse.

Mapped structure

Woodland, clearings, water and building exclusions now come from georeferenced national products. Trees grow inside coherent stands, with boundary-driven young growth. Open fields remain open rather than becoming randomly thinned parkland.

Structure preserved in playback

Stable world-cell identities, frustum-first selection, complete distant foliage, measured mip correction, 8x MSAA and matching wind/shadow/motion deformation address temporal loss. Camera paths anticipate conservative crown and scenery bounds instead of deleting trees from the line of sight.

User-directed trade-off: maximum visual quality takes precedence over live FPS. The clips are rendered offline at the correct simulation timestamps. Their smooth 30 FPS playback does not claim that Spain can deliver the same scene at 30 FPS in real time.

What this is not: an exact historical digital twin, a reconstruction of individually surveyed trees, or a claim that every asset is now photorealistic. The map is better grounded; tree identities, much understory detail, building appearance, road cross-section and rally-day weather remain modeled.

02 / Grounding & corrections

Use the actual products, not their preview colours.

The most consequential correction to the proposal was to stop treating *_ndsm.jpg as a calibrated metre-scale height raster. A plausible grayscale or coloured height preview does not establish its scale, no-data convention, legend, acquisition date or accuracy. The implementation downloads and reads the numeric Estonian GeoTIFFs, and explicitly treats Finland's source as classified height bands.

The evidence changed several earlier conclusions

No Finnish class-6 returns means no buildingsFalse. The acquired Finnish semantic product contains building footprints near the route. Absence of a class in a thinned cloud is not an absence certificate.

Dropping half the components makes a crown exactly half transparentFalse. Projected coverage, overlap, alpha filtering, viewpoint and lighting matter. The selected distant representation avoids that extra component removal.

The north–south registration issue was genuine: raw image row zero and negative world Z both denote north, but the terrain's V coordinate was inverted. Terrain, landscape sampling and grass now share z / worldDepth + 0.5. This fixes the convention; it does not turn an approximately fitted aerial mosaic into an independently surveyed orthorectification.

Data coverage above means finite numeric/classified samples within the specified belt. It is not a survey-accuracy score, a count of individual crowns, or a directly comparable denominator to the old point-cloud station-coverage audit.

03 / Engineering

One world field, shared by the scene.

01 / INPUTS

Numeric terrain and canopy; semantic vectors; Finnish height classes and inventory; existing route and imagery.

02 / WORLD FIELD

Stand footprint, height prior, validity, metric edge distance, land cover, moisture/tint and provenance.

03 / SCENE

Overstory, edge mantle, regeneration, ground/grass, mapped foundations and water, contextual road furniture.

04 / PRESERVATION

Spatial indexing, conservative bounds, stable LODs, cutout filtering, shared wind, planned cameras and measured output.

Area-based woodland and edge structure

Overstory candidates use a 3.8 m world-cell lattice with deterministic jitter and bounded density priors. A separate 2.8 m edge layer supplies shrubs, saplings and small broadleaves; a 7.5 m interior layer introduces sparse regeneration. These spacings are implementation choices, not claims that every Estonian or Finnish forest has one universal stem density. Composite sapling and shrub assets also mean an instance count is not a stem count.

Canopy height and stand boundaries determine eligibility. Water, buildings, rock, roads and the engineered road clearance exclude unsuitable placements. Source-supported isolated patches can survive in open land. The model uses a jittered area process, not a fully calibrated Thomas-process, crown-competition or silvicultural growth simulation.

Measured crown scale rather than nominal width

Meshes are normalized by height, so horizontal instance scale is not a crown diameter. The renderer measures the loaded geometry and derives scale from a desired crown span and actual model radius. The table records the 95th-percentile foliage-vertex radius and maximum radial extent. It is not a leaf-area-weighted botanical crown measurement.

Rendering that retains the intended woodland

Per-species spatial indexes restrict the query region, then conservative union bounds are frustum-culled before LOD selection. There is no nearest-N species quota silently reshuffling visible trees. Projected-size selection uses hysteresis; discontinuous seeks/cuts reset it, while a stationary pause does not force a new geometry choice. Complete authored LOD2 foliage is retained at distance, avoiding the previous derived half/quarter-component thinning.

Coverage factors are measured per texture layer and mip level, with bounded scale and an anisotropy-aware lookup. They support alpha-to-coverage and 8x MSAA; they are not an exact optical-depth invariant. Colour, shadow and velocity passes share deformation and cutout logic. Fine ground noise is attenuated when undersampled, and foliage chromatic variation no longer slides through a high-frequency world-space sine as the leaves move.

Detailed crowns fade over 340–430 m into mapped ground treatment and haze. An elevated, source-shaped canopy-sheet prototype was tested and rejected: it produced faceted, blanket-like horizons in wide views. It is not shipped or counted as a visual improvement. Proper distant volumetric/impostor crowns remain future asset work.

A separate wide-view defect came from an existing vertex-culling shortcut: connected terrain and road vertices beyond 345 m were moved individually to one clip-space point without initializing their varyings; the scenery shader had the same defect at 180 m. Mixed triangles stretched across the image. Ordinary GPU primitive clipping now preserves the surface, and the sky fills only untouched far depth. An actual GPU regression checks surface/sky ordering at 3 km.

Terrain, ground and landmarks

The broad road-height floor is removed. A local road/ditch correction ends at 16 m, with modest procedural surface displacement and separate mapped foundation/water adjustments. Vertical presentation exaggeration remains explicit. Estonia uses a denser derivative from the numeric DTM; Finland retains the existing terrain source with denser interpolation, not a newly surveyed 1 m Finnish surface.

Terrain and grass read the same woodland/moisture/tint masks. Open agricultural ground, woodland litter and moist edges therefore change together. Mapped buildings receive contained generic proxies, not automatically generated silos, tractors and companion buildings. Water follows mapped polygon boundaries; estimated levels and approximate reflections are qualified below. Signs and anti-cut bales are sparse curvature-derived illustrations, not claimed observations of the historic course.

Road wetness is derived from smoothed vertical sags and canopy shade. The GPU road surface and CPU tire emissions use the same profile. This removes the stage-independent periodic basin field without pretending to know the actual rally-day rainfall or drainage.

Camera clearance is planned, not hidden

Automatic rigs smooth the horizontal reference with acceleration/jerk checks, anticipate conservative crown and static-scenery bounds, and construct a vertically speed-bounded clearance majorant. The resulting C2 path is independent of render wall time and frame partition. A complete-route 120 Hz audit samples terrain, wind-inflated crown cylinders and building/scenery bounds before acceptance. The old independent gimbal is replaced by subject-directed aiming; manual bumper/orbit controls remain.

Conservative full crown cylinders can unnecessarily lift the camera above empty space beneath high branches. That is a deliberate safety/appearance trade-off, visible in the separately labelled native clips. It is not a proof of triangle-level collision freedom or a complete robotics planner.

05 / Temporal acceptance

Still-image quality is not the release gate.

Every formal clip has 150 individually rendered output frames at t_start + n / 30, 1920 × 1080 and constant 30 FPS for five seconds. Independent local decoding checks frame count, frame rate and duration. No repeated stills, generated intermediate frames or slowed playback were used to meet that specification.

The report players use size-bounded H264 reading copies, still 1920 × 1080, 150 frames and 30 FPS. Before and after use the same CRF24 / 4 Mbit/s maximum encoding treatment, with no retiming, interpolation, grading or geometry alteration. These compressed previews are not used for subpixel flicker measurements; linked originals and host-retained lossless frames remain the acceptance evidence.

Embedded control: Elva's stationary wind sequence retains the exact baseline camera in both versions. It helps expose unstable cutout edges, moving shadows and colour changes without confusing them with a deliberately changed flight path. Both clips are hosted on this site; the video library provides all original captures. Only the eye is stationary: wind, the car, particles and car-centred shadow coverage continue to evolve. This is not a pure wind-isolation experiment.

07 / Negative evidence & boundaries

What was rejected, qualified or left approximate.

Rejected pilot: correct trees, unsafe old camera

The first geographic candidate still put an old aerial eye inside a crown. It was not accepted as a finished improvement, and trees were not deleted to clear the lens. The new path planner addresses that presentation failure.

Revised camera: a different view, explicitly labelled

The second pilot demonstrates clearance at the same route time, but changes the eye and framing. It must not be used as evidence that a shader alone fixed the view.

Rejected far-canopy prototype and a real pause-stability defect

The first large candidate corpus passed media-format checks but failed visual review. Wide landmark shots exposed a faceted elevated canopy sheet and missing/stretched ground at the old 345 m vertex boundary. That corpus was retained, not promoted. Removing only a fragment cutoff and moving the sky to true far depth was insufficient. Fixing connected-mesh clipping removed the paused-frame instability; a separate on/off ablation then showed that the canopy sheet still looked worse than the underlying mapped terrain, so it was removed rather than disguised.

In the controlled Elva fixed-time probe, the rejected version differed by up to 105 channel levels, with 0.0702% of pixels exceeding an 8-level maximum-channel difference. After the clipping fix, eight repetitions in each of five pass ablations had maximum difference 1/255 and no pixels above that diagnostic threshold. Particle-state hashes and LOD counts stayed fixed in both versions. The final main-corpus repeated-render numbers are reported separately above; this is not a universal motion-flicker score.

Surveys and inventories span different years and are not uniformly contemporaneous with the rally replay. Finnish height classes can include power lines or other objects; nDSM includes elevated non-vegetation unless excluded by semantics. The regional species proxies are not a botanical classification of every crown.

The renderer remains a custom OpenGL raster system. Existing Vulkan car-query initialization and shader comments mentioning PCG, Lumen or ray tracing do not establish whole-scene ray-traced forest lighting. Water uses approximate environment/analytical response. Distant individual crowns beyond the detailed range and the finite mapped-extent/horizon transition remain limitations; no rejected canopy sheet is hidden in the final build. No universal photorealism percentage or unmeasured sub-millisecond cost is invented.

Accumulated rejected and superseded lossless experiments filled the host's 29 GB system volume. The encoder failed, so that attempt was not accepted. Spain was recovered, only our superseded experimental lossless files were reclaimed, and baseline/final-source lossless evidence was kept. All four derived inputs and every frozen renderer/shader/helper hash were checked before the formal candidate campaign was restarted. No renderer change was hidden in this operational recovery.

The capture tool now checks a conservative per-clip storage budget plus a 2 GB disk reserve and publishes explicit encoder-failure progress instead of leaving a stale running marker. The host restart changed the temporary tunnel hostname; the compatible viewer's default and recovery manifest were updated together.
